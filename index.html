Software Engineering

Research at the intersection of software and requirements engineering, legal compliance, and privacy. We study how requirements are elicited, formalized, verified, and validated in software systems, and build AI-assisted tools that keep what stakeholders need connected to the code that ships. The lab is directed by Dr. Mitra Bokaei Hosseini.

IEEE SANER 2024 · Rovaniemi, Finland

Code meets privacy policy at SANER 2024

In collaboration with Dr. Rocky Slavin’s lab at UTSA, Pragyan K C presented our paper “A Large-Language-Model Approach to Code and Privacy Policy Alignment” at IEEE SANER, a leading venue in software engineering. The work uses large language models to check whether apps actually do what their privacy policies say, comparing stated data practices with sensitive data leaks found through static analysis of the code.

Research

Requirements you can trust. From the crowd to compliance.

Software increasingly starts and changes through natural language: feature requests, privacy policies, regulations, and prompts that AI agents turn into code. We work on the elicitation, formalization, verification, and validation of requirements, so that what users, developers, and regulators expect stays traceable across the software lifecycle.

Current projects
01

AI-assisted change management in open source

AI-assisted tools that improve communication between stakeholders and developers, from clarifying ambiguous feature requests to tracing requests across forums, issue trackers, and code.

RE 2026 · RE 2025 · CrowdRE 2025 · CrowdRE 2026

02

Privacy by design for GenAI and IoT

Privacy-by-design frameworks for generative AI and IoT systems, built on user-centered requirements and privacy risk modeling.

Builds on SANER 2024 · PrivateNLP 2020

03

Traceability and compliance assurance

Techniques that link software artifacts to each other and to regulatory documents, such as extracting GDPR processing activities from user-authored scenarios.

ESPRE 2024 · arXiv 2024
